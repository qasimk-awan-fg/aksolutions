Group Tabs Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Group Tabs Manager keeps user-configured native browser tab groups available in normal browser windows. This policy explains how the extension handles information while providing that feature.

Information the extension handles

To maintain the tab groups that you configure, the extension accesses the current browser profile's tab, tab-group, and normal-window information. This can include tab URLs, titles, identifiers, group names, group colours, window identifiers, and the configuration you enter, such as default URLs and group placement.

Where information is kept

Configuration is kept in the browser profile using the browser's local extension storage. Temporary tab, group, and window bindings are kept in the browser's session storage. The extension does not operate a server and does not send this information outside your browser.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, share, or disclose your data to us or to third parties.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, remote code, or third-party services.
  • It does not require an account, sign-in, or payment information.
  • It does not run in Incognito or InPrivate windows.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
storageStores your configured groups and the extension's enabled/paused setting in the local browser profile.
tabsFinds, creates, moves, and restores the managed tabs needed for your configured groups.
tabGroupsCreates and maintains the native tab groups that you configure.

Your choices

You can pause enforcement at any time from the extension popup. While paused, you can remove a configured group from the options page. You can also remove the extension using your browser's extension management page; removing it clears the extension's local storage from that browser profile.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant extension update is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on the Group Tabs Manager listing in the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.
